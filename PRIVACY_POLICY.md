# Privacy Policy for Sport Amigo

**Last Updated:** October 4, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 4, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Sport Amigo** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Simon Buechi, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our Android application (`ch.simibu.sport`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide personal workout tracking, routine planning, and physical progression monitoring, Sport Amigo collects and processes the following information:

### A. Account & Authentication Information
- **Email Address and User ID**: Collected when you register or sign in via Email/Password or Google Sign-In (using Google Identity / Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication). This uniquely identifies your account and allows synchronization across sessions.
- **Display Name**: If provided via Google Sign-In, used solely to personalize greetings within the app.

### B. Health, Fitness & Workout Information
- **Workout Logs**: Exercise entries, sets, weights, repetitions, rest times, notes, and session dates/durations.
- **Routines & Templates**: Custom workout templates and target exercises created by you.
- **Physical Metrics**: Body weight, estimated body fat percentage, and circumference measurements (chest, waist, shoulders, bicep, thigh) entered manually by you for progression tracking.

### C. Technical & Diagnostic Information
- Standard technical metadata (operating system version, basic app performance) automatically processed by Google Play Services and Firebase to maintain app reliability and diagnose crashes.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:
- Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
- Store, sync, and display your workout routines, exercise logs, and body tracking history.
- Compute personal workout statistics, including 1RM (one-rep max) estimates and volume progression.
- Store user preferences locally on your device (such as language preferences and light/dark theme).

**We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal or fitness data. We do NOT display third-party advertisements or use tracking pixels/brokers.**

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

The app relies on trusted infrastructure provided by Google LLC to process authentication and cloud database operations:
- **Google Firebase Authentication**: For secure user sign-in and credential verification.
- **Google Cloud Firestore**: For cloud persistence and synchronization of workout templates, logs, and body metrics.
- **Google Play Services**: For app updates, authentication services, and platform functionality.

All data transmitted to and from Google Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) and encrypted at rest.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:  
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Your workout data is cached locally on your device (for offline accessibility) and synchronized with our secured Cloud Firestore database.
- We implement appropriate technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal and workout data for as long as your account remains active.

### Requesting Data or Account Deletion
You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time in compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion requirements:
- **In-App**: You can clear or remove individual workouts, routines, and measurements directly within the application.
- **Complete Account & Data Erasure**: To request permanent deletion of your account, authentication credentials, and all stored fitness history, please send an email to:  
  **`simon.buechi@gmail.com`** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request - Sport Amigo"*.  
  We will verify your request and permanently erase your account data within 30 days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Sport Amigo is not intended for or directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.
